## What we know

The supplied set of **five photographs** shows **two African wild dogs** walking, standing and looking toward the camera in a grassy, wooded enclosure. The location is consistent with the African wild dog exhibit at Taronga Western Plains Zoo, but the uploaded images contain **no EXIF date or GPS**, so neither the day photographed nor the individuals' names can be independently verified.

The species is **Endangered**. The *IUCN Red List 2025 assessment* estimates **1,676 mature individuals**, warns of a **decreasing** global trend, and places the number of packs globally **below 700**. Here, *mature individuals* is a technical IUCN population metric; it is **not** the total count of every wild dog, including non-breeders and juveniles. Older web pages cite approximately 6,600 adult dogs, based on older assessments. Never present those differently defined quantities as directly comparable.

## Evidence standard

- **Verified source:** an attributed and dated published zoo or scientific source.
- **Direct observation:** visible in Josh's supplied photos, without identifying individual animals.
- **Inference:** plausible from context, explicitly marked.
- **Unknown:** not established (e.g. date, keeper names, pack membership, individual age or sex).

No animal should be named or sexed based solely on coat pattern or a photograph. Zoo population news from 2023 is historical, not a reliable assertion about its composition in 2026.
