# Dubbozoo — 61 animal research checklist (1977–2026 working inventory)

**Research date:** 18 October 2026 · **Checklist:** 61 animal labels or species · **Profiles:** 61 written, 0 without a guide · **Dubbo holding unverified:** 10 · **Precise taxon/lineage not established:** 6.

> **Important:** Every previously listed entry now has a research profile, but **that does not establish that every animal ever held at Dubbo has been discovered**. Annual zoo collection/ARKS/ZIMS inventories back to the 1977 opening would be required for a comprehensive historical census. This catalogue deliberately includes historic, behind-the-scenes and provisional items.

## Status key

- **✅ Guide**: linked species ecology/conservation and local evidence profile written. This does *not* certify that the zoo currently holds the species or that the historical animal ID is correct.
- **Verified published holding**: zoo map, first-party report or NSW government account refers to the animal at Dubbo; may be historical, behind the scenes, or an exhibit.
- **⚠️ Unverified holding**: a secondary-only or combined-institution reference needs Dubbo-specific accession/keeper confirmation. This is **not** an established zoo animal even though biological research is supplied.
- **△ Taxon uncertain**: source refers to a broad label or species complex (e.g. 'Wallaby', 'Camel', 'Guineafowl', African elephants, Galápagos giant tortoises). Do not invent sub-species or Red List assessment.

### Historical completeness and interpretation

1. Request Taronga Western Plains Zoo's year-by-year animal collection/historical holdings for **1977–2026**, separating Sydney, conservation breeding, visitor exhibits and one-off veterinary patients; reconcile synonyms and transfers.
2. Cross-check records for previously omitted species, not just this inherited 61-entry list. Older zoo annual reports mention species totals around 57–65 at different dates; a **sum of unique historical species could be considerably larger**.
3. Confirm which animals are actually at Dubbo **on a particular visit**; a 2026 map label is not a realtime headcount.
4. Add animal-level provenance from published photos or Josh's originals only where supported; do not infer names/sex/health from photos.
5. For each guide, verify current IUCN Red List taxon, assessment year and population definition. Some summaries have taxonomic splits or mixed national/global status information.
6. If the evidence does not distinguish species (wallabies, camel, guineafowl, Galápagos tortoises), retain a generic *taxon unresolved* instead of splitting into confirmed zoo holdings.
