## Why 72 is a documented minimum, not a final all-time census

To establish every animal species ever *held* at the zoo since 1977, obtain Dubbo-specific annual accession and animal transaction records, including ARKS/ZIMS historical data, differentiate Taronga Sydney and Dubbo, and verify archival exhibit maps across decades. Species names, subspecies boundaries and zoo holdings change over time. Source links and evidence status are preserved in the JSON, and the separate [research audit](coverage-audit.md) tracks outstanding work. Current Red List category/assessment year has **not** been independently cross-validated for all 72 records.
