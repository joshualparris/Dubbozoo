# All-time Taronga Western Plains Zoo animal research inventory — 72 documented entries

**Historical research reviewed:** 18 October 2026 · **72/72 research guides published** · **4 historical Dubbo holdings not established** · **7 precise taxonomic identifications unresolved**.

> This is a **working historical catalogue**, **not proof that every species kept at Dubbo between 1977 and 2026 has been discovered**. A guide being written never implies a verified historic holding, current 2026 public exhibit, identified animal in Josh's photos or fully checked current IUCN assessment. The historical archive must separate Taronga Zoo Sydney from Taronga Western Plains Zoo Dubbo.

## Status key

- ✅ **Researched** means a profile linked to local evidence and species natural history exists.
- **Dated Taronga / NSW evidence** shows that species lived, was bred, or was held at Dubbo at the stated historical date, but does not show today's occupancy.
- **Historic transfer** indicates a received animal in a dated Taronga transaction.
- **Wildlife hospital only** documents rehabilitation, **not the permanent collection**.
- ⚠️ **Unverified Dubbo holding** means current evidence points only to a broader institutional, Wikipedia or anecdotal mention.
- △ **Taxon unresolved** means a map's name or older label does not establish present-day scientific subspecies/lineage.

### Important scope distinctions

- **Red kangaroo (#47):** confirmed as Dubbo veterinary rehabilitation patients, **not** a permanent breeding or display collection.
- **White-winged chough (#59) and malleefowl (#43):** documented in a behind-the-scenes conservation flight aviary.
- **Brush-tailed rock-wallaby (#37):** an actual transfer **to** Dubbo appears in the 2010–11 Taronga annual report.
- **Swamp and red-necked wallaby (#48/#55):** both historically confirmed in Dubbo's Aussie Walkthrough, although contemporary visitor maps collapse them to a 'Wallaby' label.
- **Southern hairy-nosed wombat (#53):** published Taronga breeding material cited previously is **Sydney-specific**, not proof of Dubbo.
- **White-browed woodswallow (#58):** species-management and transfers at Taronga-wide scale do not give verified Dubbo provenance.

### What remains to claim 'every animal ever'

Taronga opened Western Plains Zoo in 1977. Animal numbers and taxonomic names have changed across nearly five decades. To generate a definitive all-time list requires annual Dubbo-only collection/transfer reports or historical ARKS/ZIMS records, plus original exhibit maps and clarification of wild visitors, sanctuary breeding, patient wildlife and genuinely owned collection animals. Public sources can continue to expand this *documented subset*, but **72 is not certified exhaustive**.

For reproducible structured status and source links: [72-entry machine-readable inventory](../data/all-time-animal-inventory.json), [the evidence audit](coverage-audit.md).
