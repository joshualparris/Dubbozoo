# All-time Dubbo Zoo animal inventory — numbered research checklist

**Audited:** 18 October 2026 · **Scope:** animal names with published evidence of being held, displayed, bred or managed at **Taronga Western Plains Zoo in Dubbo** since 1977, including conservation and historical holdings. **This is not a complete 1977–2026 animal register.** It excludes one-off wildlife patients, wild visitors, and animals merely in Taronga Sydney unless specifically traced to Dubbo.

**61 distinct checklist entries: 15 with deep species guides, 46 not yet deeply researched. 12 entries presently rely only on a secondary list and require primary confirmation.** A name on a map, including a current map, does not independently prove it is alive or on show in October 2026.

## Reading the list
- ✅ Detailed `docs/` research guide already exists. Photo records are separate and six animal groups now have user-supplied photos.
- ⬜ No deep guide yet, even if the species has an official zoo mention.
- ⚠️ **Secondary-list only** or exact taxon unverified; check against Taronga's archival holdings.
- **Historical/sanctuary** means on premises previously, behind the scenes or under conservation management, **not necessarily on public display today**.

**Documentation hierarchy:** [Taronga 2026 map](https://www.taronga.org.au/dubbo-zoo/map), [Taronga 2024 map](https://taronga.org.au/sites/default/files/2024-03/24-039%20TWPZ%20Map%20ED17v04_EASTERAUTUMNSH_FA_OTL_MAP%20ONLY.pdf), [Taronga Dubbo directory](https://www.taronga.org.au/wildlife-and-conservation/animals/dubbo), [2024–25 Taronga annual report](https://www.taronga.org.au/siteassets/about/polices--publications/annual-reports/Taronga_AnnualReport_2024_25.pdf), and other dated first-party records linked per animal.

## Known records not double-counted

- **Sri Lankan elephant** is not a separate entry from **Asian elephant** here; Taronga's documents describe origins/lineages within the elephant programme. A geographic origin does not by itself establish a separate species.
- **Pygmy goats** are a domestic-goat type, not a separate species entry.
- **Black-rhino/cheetah subspecies** in some lists are part of the parent entries, pending taxonomic verification.
- **Eastern grey kangaroos roaming wild on zoo grounds** are not counted as managed zoo animals. Their wild presence is not evidence of a captive collection.
- **One-off wildlife hospital patients** (e.g. rescued wombats) and unconfirmed species named only in combined Sydney-and-Dubbo reporting are excluded unless linked directly to Dubbo collection history.
- This list mixes species names and a small number of unresolved broad exhibit labels, and thus **61 is a catalogue-entry count, not a validated biological species count**.

## To establish a genuine *all-ever* list

Ask Taronga's records/archive team for annual **animal collection/holdings inventory or ZIMS/ARKS historic species reports**, separated by **Dubbo versus Sydney**, spanning 1977–2026. Exclude one-time clinical patients or identify them separately. Cross-reference historical maps, accession/transfer records, breeding programmes and taxonomy synonym changes. Confirm the `12` secondary-only entries, exact wallaby/camel/guineafowl taxa, and whether each species was displayed or held privately. Then update each entry's first/last holding dates and current status without pretending today's visitor map is the history of every animal ever kept.
