# Archival verification request — historic animals at Taronga Western Plains Zoo

**Prepared:** 18 October 2026 · **Status:** **not sent** — research correspondence draft, suitable for a human to send to Taronga's archive/collections team or library.

## Why this research is needed

The independent Dubbozoo historical field journal [currently records 72 researched entries](all-time-animal-inventory.md), but **cannot claim it is a complete list of all animals ever housed in the Dubbo zoo** since opening in 1977. Public visitor maps and Taronga-wide annual reports do not comprehensively identify site-specific entries, transfer dates and all species historically kept behind the scenes.

## Draft archival enquiry

**Subject: Historical animal collection inventory — Taronga Western Plains Zoo Dubbo (1977–2026)**

Hello Taronga Collections / Records team,

I'm compiling a documented, independent historical wildlife field journal about Taronga Western Plains Zoo at Dubbo. I've been tracing the animals kept at the Dubbo site since its 1977 opening, including past public exhibits, conservation breeding facilities and animal movements.

Would you be able to advise whether there is an archive or available report that lists the species held at **Western Plains Zoo / Taronga Western Plains Zoo, Dubbo**, by year, between 1977 and 2026?

The most useful records would be historical **ARKS/ZIMS species-holdings summaries**, yearly accession/transfer logs at species level, past exhibit maps/printed visitor guides, and archives of species programmes. A species-level summary is sufficient: no private staff or sensitive individual records are needed.

My working list currently has 72 research entries. I'm especially trying to verify if the following were *managed Dubbo animals*, rather than free-ranging wildlife or records from Taronga Zoo Sydney:

1. Indian peafowl (*Pavo cristatus*) — were any accessioned to the zoo, or were they free-ranging birds?
2. Smooth yabby (*Cherax destructor*) — was there a formal holding or visitor/aquarium exhibit?
3. Southern hairy-nosed wombat (*Lasiorhinus latifrons*) — Taronga publications document wombats at **Sydney**. Were any housed at Dubbo, and when?
4. White-browed woodswallow (*Artamus superciliosus*) — historic Taronga-wide animal transfers appear in annual reports, but I cannot establish Dubbo as the holding site.

I'm also trying to establish the exact taxon/lineage of: historical African elephants; Guineafowl; Galápagos giant tortoises; African forest buffalo; animals historically labelled Bengal tiger; and historical dingoes. **Arabian dromedary camels are now confirmed from Taronga keeper Ruby Cullen's 2023 profile of Samera**.

If there are restrictions on supplying archival inventories, could you point me towards the relevant catalogue, collection librarian or published years/sections?

I will attribute and link to Taronga's original publications and clearly distinguish verified evidence from remaining uncertainties.

Thank you for any guidance.

Regards,
Dubbozoo research project
https://github.com/joshualparris/Dubbozoo

## Libraries and archives worth checking

- [Taronga institutional history](https://www.taronga.org.au/about/our-history): zoo opening and major breeding initiatives.
- [Taronga annual report 2014–15](https://taronga.org.au/sites/default/files/content/pdf/Taronga_Annual_Report_2014-15.pdf): Appendix 3 tables with institution columns **TZ** (Sydney) and **TWPZ** (Dubbo), and Appendix 4 transfer movements.
- [Taronga annual report 2010–11](https://www.taronga.org.au/sites/default/files/content/pdf/Taronga_Annual_Report_2010-11.pdf): Appendix 4 includes a brush-tailed rock-wallaby transfer to Dubbo.
- [NSW Parliament Hansard, 25 June 2003](https://www.parliament.nsw.gov.au/parliamentary-business/hansard/hansard-full-details?id=HANSARD-1323879322-62557&section=HANSARD-1323879322-92247): recollection of historical Bengal tiger and dingo exhibits during 1979–84.
- [State Library NSW tourism ephemera, Box 40 Folder 6 Item 15](https://content-lists.sl.nsw.gov.au/tabular-list/ephemera-tourism-box-40-2010-2019-1): original 'Western Plains Zoo Dubbo, NSW' folded pamphlet/map, date unspecified in catalogue (requires viewing).
- [Powerhouse Dahl and Geoffrey Collings design archive](https://collection.powerhouse.com.au/object/123616): historical zoo guide reportedly in archive; confirm exact object before assigning species.
- [1997 zoo map discussed by historical enthusiasts](https://www.zoochat.com/community/threads/taronga-western-plains-zoo-history.466494/page-2): secondary lead only. Acquire original source image/map with publication provenance.

## Acceptance criteria for all-ever inventory

For each added animal: a taxon name, **Dubbo-specific** dated zoo reference (not just a Sydney programme), record category (exhibit, breeding/conservation, hospital patient, wild visitor or unconfirmed), first/last year supported and whether a scientific subspecies ID can be proven. Never infer current 2026 animals from historic maps or names; never use the date a scanned article was republished as the original event date.
