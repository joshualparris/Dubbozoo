# The 1995–1999 Dubbo Zoo archive breakthrough

**Reviewed:** 18 October 2026. **Purpose:** turn a set of newly discovered historical leads into responsibly graded research. **Not an authenticated formal Taronga inventory**. [Numbered species catalogue](all-time-animal-inventory.md).

## Origin and chain of custody

A ZooChat member publishing under 'akasha' in **June 2026** posted a transcription of a childhood school diary claiming to have been written on **Saturday 6 May 1995** after a family zoo visit. The user subsequently uploaded many scanned/digitised **July 1999** analogue photographs from the same family visits. [Diary and photo retrospective](https://www.zoochat.com/community/threads/taronga-western-plains-zoo-1995-visit.497022/) · [1999 zoo photo index](https://www.zoochat.com/community/media/categories/taronga-western-plains-zoo.504/).

**Caution on dates:** a digital JPEG may have EXIF identifying the **2026 iPhone scanning or upload date**, not the date the *film picture* was captured. The uploader's dating of May 1995 and July 1999 is historical testimony; it has not been independently verified from original notebooks, negatives or zoo admissions. The same author offers a modern interpretation of childhood labels that should not be silently treated as a 1995 keeper sign.

## A distinct historic individual: Chester, the white tiger

The [Western Australian Government media statement of 7 December 1995](https://www.wa.gov.au/government/media-statements/Court%20Coalition%20Government/Visit-of-rare-white-tiger-to-Perth-Zoo-19951207) confirms Chester visited Dubbo before moving on loan to Perth Zoo. This is one of the strongest original-government species-and-individual sources in this batch. **White tiger is coat colour, not a separate species**; it is therefore added as an exhibit episode in the [historic tiger guide](species/bengal-tiger-historical.md), not a 90th species.

The visitor's description of historical **'Asiatic Lions'** is also interesting. The [2008 zoo-community discussion](https://www.zoochat.com/community/threads/asiastic-lions-in-australia.15749/) contains conflicting explanations of imported hybrids and breeding ancestry. It cannot establish **pure Asiatic lion genetics** or an additional lion species. [Lion guide addendum](african-lion.md).

## Excluded or unproven leads

1. **Giant anteater:** a commentator reviewing a December 1997 zoo map remembers it being **listed** although they had *never seen one*. This is a **planned/advertised label, not proof of actual Dubbo holding**. [1997 map discussion](https://www.zoochat.com/community/threads/taronga-western-plains-zoo-history.466494/page-2). Do not create a 'verified giant anteater' row yet.
2. **Wildebeest:** the 1995 child diary uses a colloquial 'wilderbeasts' label. Independent readers suggest **African forest buffalo** in the same paddock, but disagree. There is no authenticated wildebeest accession here; **do not double-count an unverified second species**.
3. **Diamond python / wood duck / ibis:** the journal author's additional sketches do not demonstrate captive exhibit ownership. The author believed birds were wild and could not identify where a python was kept; further corroboration required.
4. **Bettongs / 'rat-kangaroos' in children's zoo:** the visitor's memory of small exhibits is **not sufficiently specific** for a species-level entry.
5. **Nilgai, chital, sambar, capybara and macropods:** useful historical visitor evidence, **not** automatic proof of zoo ownership, precise captive taxon or continuing populations.
6. **2026 species-list status:** a [January 2026 external visitor account](https://www.zoochat.com/community/threads/taronga-western-plains-zoo-january-2026.495969/) reports white-handed gibbon and Persian onager off-display/no longer signed; these are **visitation observations**, not evidence of transfer, death or total programme closure.

## IUCN Red List change: mala

IUCN's official [2025-1 category change table](https://nc.iucnredlist.org/redlist/content/attachment_files/2025-1_RL_Table_7.pdf) and [2025-2 aggregate table](https://nc.iucnredlist.org/redlist/content/attachment_files/2025-2_RL_Table7.pdf) expressly list *Lagorchestes hirsutus* changing from **Vulnerable to Near Threatened**, marked **G** (genuine change) in the 2025-1 release. This is the worldwide IUCN extinction-risk category; separate Australian national or territory laws may still categorise the species differently. The species-level detailed 2025 assessment was **not opened**. Record this as an **IUCN primary category-change table**, not a directly read species-level assessment.
