**Updated:** 18 October 2026 · **10 previously retrieved taxon-matched IUCN assessments** · **8 further IUCN species records returned in the search index (full-page fetch unavailable)** · **one directly sourced IUCN category-change table** · **1 latest-IUCN-category pointer on an older official page** · **1 official IUCN news confirmation** · **5 other graded citations** · **5 unresolved/domestic types** · **58 without a taxon-specific assessment cross-check**.

> **Not all 89 animals have independently confirmed *current* IUCN assessments.** The scientific rank, assessed date, year published, source grade and global *wild-population trend* are distinct fields. A zoo-bred or rescued animal count cannot be substituted for wild-population data. Even an IUCN page returned in search may point to newer revisions.

## Corrected and newly located conservation facts

- **Q0007 African savanna elephant:** *Loxodonta africana* [IUCN 2022 amended assessment](https://doi.org/10.2305/IUCN.UK.2022-2.RLTS.T181008073A223031019.en) **Endangered, decreasing**. The separate *L. cyclotis* is [Critically Endangered, decreasing](https://doi.org/10.2305/IUCN.UK.2021-1.RLTS.T181007989A181019888.en). [2025 improved forest DNA surveys](https://iucn.org/press-release/202511/dna-methods-help-uncover-further-african-forest-elephants-species-remains) should not be mistaken for recovery.
- **Banteng:** older [IUCN 2016 assessment](https://doi.org/10.2305/IUCN.UK.2016-2.RLTS.T2888A46362970.en) was **EN/decreasing** but its **latest assessment** field reports **2025 Critically Endangered**; [2026 peer-reviewed report](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1617138126001342) corroborates CR. **The 2025 full assessment's trend and criteria still require direct checking.**
- **Black rhino:** IUCN reports [Critically Endangered despite a slow rise in wild numbers over 2012–2018](https://iucn.org/news/species/202003/conservation-efforts-bring-cautious-hope-african-rhinos-iucn-red-list). CR is an extinction-risk category, not proof of continuous year-on-year decline.
- **Common hippopotamus:** [IUCN 2017 taxon account](https://doi.org/10.2305/IUCN.UK.2017-2.RLTS.T10103A18567364.en) **Vulnerable, overall trend Stable**, notwithstanding regional declines.
- **Barbary sheep:** [IUCN 2022](https://doi.org/10.2305/IUCN.UK.2022-1.RLTS.T1151A214430287.en) **Vulnerable, decreasing**, 5,000–10,000 mature wild individuals at assessment time.
- **Black-and-white ruffed lemur:** [IUCN species record 2020](https://doi.org/10.2305/IUCN.UK.2020-2.RLTS.T22918A115574178.en) **Critically Endangered, decreasing**.
- **Asian small-clawed otter:** [IUCN 2021](https://doi.org/10.2305/IUCN.UK.2021-3.RLTS.T44166A164580923.en) **Vulnerable, decreasing**.
- **Ring-tailed lemur:** [IUCN 2020](https://doi.org/10.2305/IUCN.UK.2020-2.RLTS.T11496A115565760.en) **Endangered, decreasing**.
- **Leopard tortoise:** [IUCN 2022 amended](https://doi.org/10.2305/IUCN.UK.2022-1.RLTS.T163449A217761530.en) **Least Concern, trend Unknown**, not Stable.
- **Common ostrich:** older [IUCN 2018](https://doi.org/10.2305/IUCN.UK.2018-2.RLTS.T45020636A132189458.en) **Least Concern, decreasing**; it points to newer 2025 LC, detailed trend not separately reviewed.
- **Mala:** [IUCN 2025 change table](https://nc.iucnredlist.org/redlist/content/attachment_files/2025-1_RL_Table_7.pdf) changes global classification from VU to **Near Threatened (genuine change)**, not necessarily a change in Australian statutory protection.
- **Southern hairy-nosed wombat:** [IUCN 2025](https://doi.org/10.2305/IUCN.UK.2025-1.RLTS.T40555A258654451.en) **Least Concern** (older NT obsolete).
- **Plains zebra:** [IUCN 2016](https://doi.org/10.2305/IUCN.UK.2016-2.RLTS.T41013A45172424.en) **Near Threatened, decreasing**; zoo's LC text is not matching this assessment.
- **White rhinoceros:** [IUCN 2020](https://doi.org/10.2305/IUCN.UK.2020-1.RLTS.T4185A45813880.en) **Near Threatened at species level**, separate from northern white subspecies.

## Why unresolved source work remains

1. **58 records have no independently examined same-taxon assessment**, while some existing taxon pages are superseded and a subset of IUCN search excerpts could not be opened in full.
2. Domestic pygmy goat, domestic water buffalo, dromedary and guinea pig must not inherit an endangered wild relative's IUCN category; unlabeled Galápagos island tortoise has no single reliable island-level rank.
3. For each additional assessment: store official taxon identifier, current published edition, assessor, date assessed, trend field and criteria, plus source URL. Keep Australian EPBC/NSW legal status in independent columns.
4. The **622-question atlas is not automatically certified** merely because an answer contains a URL. Dated keeper records, original photos and a full historical animal register are still missing.
