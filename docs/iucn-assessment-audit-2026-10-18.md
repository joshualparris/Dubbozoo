# Conservation-status provenance audit — 18 October 2026

The current [IUCN Red List is version 2026-1](https://api.iucnredlist.org/). **A guide being written is not proof its conservation category is current.** This register is deliberately conservative: 10 primary IUCN assessment records checked; 3 entries have announcement or secondary evidence; 4 are not assignable as one straightforward wild species; and 59 still need direct assessment checks.

**CAUTION:** Some checked pages mention a **later** assessment than the individual URL opened; 'direct assessment checked' **does not automatically mean confirmed latest version**. Confirm the newest IUCN result for the same taxonomic rank before making a headline in the app. Dates below distinguish **assessment date** from **publication year**.

## Important corrected statuses

1. Southern hairy-nosed wombat (*Lasiorhinus latifrons*): **Least Concern (2025 assessment publication)**, replacing the historic Near Threatened 2016 status. [IUCN primary](https://doi.org/10.2305/IUCN.UK.2025-1.RLTS.T40555A258654451.en).
2. Plains zebra (*Equus quagga*): **Near Threatened** in [IUCN's 2016 species assessment](https://doi.org/10.2305/IUCN.UK.2016-2.RLTS.T41013A45172424.en); *Equus burchellii* appears as a synonym. Taronga's 'Least Concern' page does not supersede an IUCN species assessment.
3. White rhinoceros (*Ceratotherium simum*): **Near Threatened at full species level**, [IUCN 2020](https://doi.org/10.2305/IUCN.UK.2020-1.RLTS.T4185A45813880.en), distinct from northern-white-rhino conservation claims.
4. Northern hairy-nosed wombat (*Lasiorhinus krefftii*): **Critically Endangered** in a 2016-published [IUCN assessment](https://doi.org/10.2305/IUCN.UK.2016-2.RLTS.T11343A21959050.en), **explicitly needs updating**; 2015 wild population metrics are not 2026 figures.
5. African savanna elephant (*Loxodonta africana*): **Endangered**, IUCN [2022 amended record](https://doi.org/10.2305/IUCN.UK.2022-2.RLTS.T181008073A223031019.en); African forest elephant (*L. cyclotis*) has a **separate CR** IUCN assessment. Dubbo historically held savanna species per original zoo/tribunal documentation.
6. Scimitar-horned oryx (*Oryx dammah*): the [2023 IUCN announcement](https://iucn.org/press-release/202312/freshwater-fish-highlight-escalating-climate-impacts-species-iucn-red-list) says **Endangered** after reintroduction, not Extinct in the Wild or Critically Endangered.

## Correct interpretation

- **LC, NT, VU, EN, CR, EW, EX** are categories assessed for specific taxa at defined dates. Taxonomic splits, legal statuses and regional classifications differ.
- **NE / DD** and 'not checked' are different things: no entry is assigned official NE or DD simply because this research has not checked it.
- Do not attach an IUCN global status for a wild species to **domestic pygmy goats, domestic dromedary camels or domesticated water buffalo**. 'Galápagos tortoise' needs island/lineage clarification first.
- Do not use a 2025 Green Status of Species assessment as if it were a new 2025 Red List extinction-risk assessment. They measure different things.
- Population statistics must store **survey/estimation year, wild/captive status, geography and all-ages versus mature individuals**; numbers in this summary should not be compared without checking those definitions.
- For future automation: validate **taxon name + taxon rank + newest assessment record**, retain previous assessments in version history, and publish an 'updated on' date only after actually reading the IUCN record. An API token/permissions may be required; do not scrape protected endpoints.
