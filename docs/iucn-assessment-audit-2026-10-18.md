## Material scientific corrections

- **Mala / rufous hare-wallaby (*Lagorchestes hirsutus*): Near Threatened** in the [official IUCN 2025-1 change table](https://nc.iucnredlist.org/redlist/content/attachment_files/2025-1_RL_Table_7.pdf), changed from **Vulnerable** and marked a *genuine* category change. Detailed individual assessment not opened.
- **Southern hairy-nosed wombat (*Lasiorhinus latifrons*): Least Concern 2025**, replacing historic Near Threatened. [IUCN assessment](https://doi.org/10.2305/IUCN.UK.2025-1.RLTS.T40555A258654451.en).
- **Plains zebra (*Equus quagga*): Near Threatened** in [IUCN 2016](https://doi.org/10.2305/IUCN.UK.2016-2.RLTS.T41013A45172424.en); newer Taronga public wording conflicts with this taxon-matched dated assessment.
- **White rhinoceros (*Ceratotherium simum*): Near Threatened** species-level [IUCN 2020](https://doi.org/10.2305/IUCN.UK.2020-1.RLTS.T4185A45813880.en); do not substitute northern-subspecies status.
- **Scimitar-horned oryx (*Oryx dammah*): Endangered** following reintroduction per [IUCN 2023 announcement](https://iucn.org/press-release/202312/freshwater-fish-highlight-escalating-climate-impacts-species-iucn-red-list); not the earlier Extinct in the Wild classification.
- **Common wombat (*Vombatus ursinus*): LC** in its [IUCN assessors' 2025 university publication record](https://researchers.westernsydney.edu.au/en/publications/vombatus-ursinus-the-iucn-red-list-of-threatened-species-2025/), not a directly fetched portal page.
- **Diamond firetail (*Stagonopleura guttata*): VU** per [ACT 2024 conservation advice citing IUCN/BirdLife 2022](https://legislation.act.gov.au/DownloadFile/ni/2024-251/current/PDF/2024-251.PDF), not verified against a later portal record.
- Historical Dubbo African savanna elephants (*Loxodonta africana*) and forest elephants (*L. cyclotis*) are not the same Red List taxon.

## Rules for subsequent source updates

1. Check the **exact scientific taxon and rank**, the latest source record, date assessed and year published. Giraffe/tiger subspecies splits and historical captive mixed lineage especially need caution.
2. Keep IUCN **global extinction risk**, NSW legislation, EPBC national law, and zoo-managed population separate. A status for wild species cannot be projected onto a domestic goat/guinea pig/camel.
3. Distinguish a direct **full species Red List assessment** from IUCN aggregate change tables, IUCN press notes, peer-reviewed citations and government secondary references. The 2025 mala change is direct *IUCN tabular evidence*, **not** a directly read individual species assessment.
4. Do not treat 'not independently checked' as formal IUCN categories Data Deficient (DD) or Not Evaluated (NE).
5. Don't compare different survey years, mature breeding individuals, all wild ages, captive zoo individuals or reintroduced populations as equivalent.
6. Preserve assessment history and source DOI as well as the newest confirmed assessment if a newer one is independently read. IUCN Green Status of Species is a different measure from Red List extinction risk.
