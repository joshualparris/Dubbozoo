# African forest buffalo (historical) — historical Dubbo animal research

**Scientific name / historical classification:** *Syncerus caffer nanus (classification varies)*  
**Region:** Africa · **Dubbo evidence:** historical first-party / official record · **Reviewed:** 18 October 2026.

## Historic Dubbo evidence

Taronga's historical press release about hippo Mana explicitly names Forest Buffalo in the African Savannah mixed-species enclosure.

## Identification and scientific distinctions

A generally smaller forest-dwelling African buffalo form, often reddish-brown, with shorter less broad horns than many savannah buffalo. Forest buffalo taxonomy is unsettled; historical zoo label does not establish genetic ancestry.

## Natural history and ecological function

Uses tropical forest clearings, forest edges and moist grassland, taking sedges, grasses and browse. Social group size varies with clearing availability and visibility.

## Conservation and research limitations

Hunting, disease from livestock and forest fragmentation may affect local populations. Don't assume a separate IUCN assessment exists for each historically named buffalo subspecies; check the taxonomy and assessment scope.

Scientific status must be matched to the **taxon, assessment date and population unit**. Historic zoo breeding records demonstrate care, not improved wild populations. The exact species/subspecies or purity of historical animals may be uncertain without acquisition or breeding registers.
