# Asian water buffalo (domesticated) — Dubbozoo species research

**Inventory number:** 26/61 · **Scientific identification:** *Bubalus bubalis; not automatically the wild water buffalo Bubalus arnee* · **Conservation:** Taronga lists the domestic taxon as 'Unlisted'; wild water buffalo is a distinct conservation issue.  
**Research reviewed:** 18 October 2026. This is **not** a Josh-supplied photo observation.

## Identification, taxonomy and range

Very large horned buffalo with heavy body, spreading horns, wide feet and habit of bathing in mud and water. It differs from an American bison, which has a prominent shoulder hump and shaggy upper body.

Taxonomic names, local subspecies, domestic forms and conservation categories must align. Where the old zoo map uses a broad label or conflicting name, keep uncertainty until a dated Taronga animal-holdings record verifies the taxon.

## Diet, behaviour and ecology

Feeds on grasses and aquatic plants in floodplains, wetlands and managed pastures; mud wallowing helps heat regulation and ectoparasite relief. Domesticated water buffalo have been used in agriculture for transport and ploughing as well as dairy and meat.

## Conservation ecology and threats

Feral populations can affect wetlands where introduced. They must not be treated as wild endangered Asian water buffalo; conservation needs are different.

**Assessment limit:** Taronga lists the domestic taxon as 'Unlisted'; wild water buffalo is a distinct conservation issue. Wild population figures require a publication year and explicit denominator (mature breeders versus all ages). Zoo populations and reintroduced herds must not be added to wild estimates without a method that does so.

## Taronga Western Plains Zoo connection

Other buffalo may remain, but a current individual census was not verified.
