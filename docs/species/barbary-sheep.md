## Species identity and identification

A stout caprine with long pale throat/chest hair, especially conspicuous on mature males, and backward-sweeping horns. Despite the English name it is often treated as a goat-antelope rather than a true domestic sheep.

**Native range / habitat:** Rocky hills and dry mountains of North Africa; introduced feral populations occur elsewhere. Where the exhibit label lacks a confirmed scientific identification, this description is **conditional** on future species verification.

## Feeding, behaviour and natural history

Climbs steep rocky slopes, using secure footing, cover and crevices; feeds on shrubs, grasses and herbs, often shifting with seasons. Group sizes vary and males can compete through horn displays.

## Wild conservation and scientific interpretation

Hunting, habitat competition and fragmentation affect parts of its native range; introduced populations create separate management questions and should not be counted as native-range conservation recovery.
