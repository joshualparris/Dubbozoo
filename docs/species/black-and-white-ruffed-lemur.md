## Species identity and identification

A large lemur with strongly contrasting black and white fur, a long bushy tail, and ruffs of hair around the ears and neck. Color pattern varies among geographic forms; visual pattern alone does not prove subspecies.

**Native range / habitat:** Eastern Madagascar's tropical rainforests. Where the exhibit label lacks a confirmed scientific identification, this description is **conditional** on future species verification.

## Feeding, behaviour and natural history

Frugivorous arboreal primate that also uses nectar, flowers and leaves. As a large fruit eater it can disperse seeds; it may act as an important pollinator of certain flowers. Group size and spacing respond to seasonal fruit supply.

**Field question:** How might a large fruit-eating lemur help regrow a rainforest? Look for an observable feature or action rather than assuming an unseen purpose, personality, sex, health or family relationship from a single image.

## Wild conservation and scientific interpretation

Forest clearance, fragmentation and hunting in Madagascar. Fragmented remnant forests reduce food availability and opportunities for population connectivity.
