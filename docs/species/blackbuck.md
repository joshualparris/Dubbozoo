# Blackbuck — Dubbozoo species research

**Inventory number:** 28/61 · **Scientific identification:** *Antilope cervicapra* · **Conservation:** Least Concern globally after recovery in parts of India; verify latest assessment.  
**Research reviewed:** 18 October 2026. This is **not** a Josh-supplied photo observation.

## Identification, taxonomy and range

Graceful South Asian antelope with prominent spiral horns usually in adult males and strong sexual coat differences; females and younger animals often tawny and hornless. Horn presence alone is not a definitive age or sex indicator.

Taxonomic names, local subspecies, domestic forms and conservation categories must align. Where the old zoo map uses a broad label or conflicting name, keep uncertainty until a dated Taronga animal-holdings record verifies the taxon.

## Diet, behaviour and ecology

Feeds on grasses and forbs in open Indian subcontinent grasslands and agricultural mosaics. Males can establish display territories (leks) during breeding and herds may move as rainfall and food supply shift.

Research prompt: How does a grassland specialist cope when grazing areas become farmland? When recording an observed animal, state the count, surroundings and directly seen behaviour rather than extrapolating kinship or medical condition.

## Conservation ecology and threats

Habitat loss from grassland conversion and fencing, dog attacks and conflict on farmland can threaten local populations even when overall IUCN status is lower risk.

**Assessment limit:** Least Concern globally after recovery in parts of India; verify latest assessment. Wild population figures require a publication year and explicit denominator (mature breeders versus all ages). Zoo populations and reintroduced herds must not be added to wild estimates without a method that does so.

## Taronga Western Plains Zoo connection

Taronga's April 2026 post reports **twelve blackbuck calves** and the Waterhole precinct's older release explicitly places blackbuck herds alongside zebra. Historical blackbuck transfers prompted Taronga to revise its welfare inspection and transfer procedures; this documented governance issue should not be omitted.

**Evidence policy:** published Taronga reports are dated records, not a live animal database. The exhibit location, keeper-talk time, current animal names, breeding status and count should be checked before a future visit; no personal sighting is asserted in this guide.
