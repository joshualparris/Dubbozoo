# Diamond firetail — historical Dubbo aviary evidence

**Scientific name:** *Stagonopleura guttata* · **Evidence:** 2009 photographic visitor account of a Dubbo zoo walk-through aviary, not yet independently keeper-verified.

## Historic Dubbo connection

A [2009 visitor photo travelogue of Western Plains Zoo Dubbo](https://www.australiasomuchtosee.com/2009dubbozoo2.htm) captions a **Diamond Firetail Finch in the zoo's walk-through aviary**, alongside a Superb Parrot. This is stronger than merely finding the bird wild near the park and suggests a managed aviary context, but **a photographed bird in an aviary does not alone prove a dated zoo acquisition or the length of its holding**.

## Identification and life history

A compact native Australian estrildid finch with distinctive red bill/rump, spotted dark flanks, light underside and black chest band. Adult sexes look similar. Found in southeastern temperate grass/woodland country; primarily grass seed eater with some other dietary components linked to season and breeding. Nesting requires woody cover and grass structure. It is not the same species as the Australian Zebra Finch, with which it may share captivity or broad habitat.

## Conservation

Loss of grassy woodland, woodland fragmentation and decline in native grasses/seed resources are concerns, along with nest predation. Legal listings and IUCN rank vary: do **not** infer a current global IUCN category merely from Australian threatened species legislation. Link to a dated species-matched authority once verified.
