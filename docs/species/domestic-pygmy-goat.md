# Domestic pygmy goat — Dubbozoo research guide

## Scientific identification and adaptations

Small sturdy domesticated caprine with cloven hooves and variable coat colours; pygmy denotes body/breed type, not a separate species. Both sexes may have horns, so horns alone do not assign sex.

## Food, reproduction and ecological roles

Browses leaves, woody shoots and pasture. Highly social, learning and communicating through body posture and calls. Care requires suitable diet/minerals, space, company and hoof management; goats are not rubbish disposals.

**Field question:** Why is a pygmy goat an animal-breed record instead of a unique wild conservation taxon? Record time, location, number seen and directly observable actions without guessing health, social status or individual name.

## Threats, conservation and scientific limitations

Conservation category of a domestic breed is a livestock-diversity matter distinct from Red List status of wild goats. Never claim wildlife reintroduction for small domestic goats.

The assessment shown here is a dated conservation/legal context, **not a fresh population estimate**. Legal rankings in NSW, federal Australia and the IUCN may differ; wild, domestic, conservation-breeding and hospital animals must remain separate. For unknown taxonomy, no precise species-level Red List label should be assigned until confirmed.

## Specific Dubbo zoo history / verification status

The **April 2026 Dubbo map** explicitly labels Pygmy Goats. A **2008 historic Taronga release** additionally describes a male goat kid as temporary companion to a hand-reared scimitar-horned oryx. That calf is not necessarily an ancestor of current goats; do not merge separate holdings.

**Interpretation boundary:** exhibition, zoo-managed conservation breeding, temporary wildlife rehabilitation and incidental animals roaming zoo land are **four different categories**. The historical checklist should not imply a public sighting, photograph, current presence or persistent zoo ownership without dated supporting records.
