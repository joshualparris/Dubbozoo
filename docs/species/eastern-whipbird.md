## Physical identification and life history

Secretive olive-green ground-foraging bird with long dark tail, dark head and white cheek patch. The loud 'whipcrack' call often consists of paired male/female phrases, but a recording is necessary to claim a particular duet.

## Food web, habitat and behavioural ecology

Uses dense eastern Australian forest and wet understorey, scratching through leaf litter for insects and small invertebrates. Dense low vegetation is a vital refuge from predators and for nesting.
