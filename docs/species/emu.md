# Emu — researched species guide

**Dubbozoo inventory #39 · Taxon:** *Dromaius novaehollandiae* · **Conservation:** Least Concern broadly, current assessment version to verify.  
**Sources reviewed:** 18 October 2026. **Observations:** no supplied photo directly documents this species.

## Physical identification and life history

Australia's tallest living native bird, flightless, brown shaggy plumage, long powerful legs and three toes on each foot. Do not confuse it with the African ostrich, which has two toes.

## Food web, habitat and behavioural ecology

Omnivore feeding on seeds, fruits, shoots and insects. Emus can travel widely with rainfall, spreading seeds; breeding often involves a male incubating eggs and guarding chicks for months.

## Risks and conservation actions

Regional habitat loss, road collisions and barriers to movement, rather than a uniformly endangered national population. Distinguish wild emus from captive managed exhibits.

**Status caveat:** Least Concern broadly, current assessment version to verify. National/state legal listings and IUCN global assessments may differ. Zoo conservation work should be judged by genetic diversity and wild survival outcomes, not only births.
