# Greater bilby — researched species guide

**Dubbozoo inventory #40 · Taxon:** *Macrotis lagotis* · **Conservation:** Vulnerable under Australian law (IUCN status/date separate).  
**Sources reviewed:** 18 October 2026. **Observations:** no supplied photo directly documents this species.

## Physical identification and life history

Nocturnal desert marsupial with very long ears, pointed muzzle, black-and-white tail, soft fur and sturdy digging foreclaws. It is a rabbit-eared bandicoot relative, not a rabbit or rodent.

## Food web, habitat and behavioural ecology

Digs deep spiral burrows that provide cool shelter; forages for bulbs, seeds, insects and larvae, turning soil and creating microhabitats for plants and other animals. Breeds rapidly under favourable conditions but survival is constrained by predators and drought.

## Risks and conservation actions

Introduced cats and foxes, grazing change, fire regimes and habitat disturbance. Fenced sanctuaries are valuable insurance but reintroduction success beyond fences needs monitoring of predation and breeding.

## Dubbo zoo's verified role / limitations

Taronga established a **110-hectare predator-proof Sanctuary in 2019** with 18 founding bilbies. Its **15 April 2025** report said the free-ranging sanctuary population exceeded **130** and described health checks; these are dated facility counts. Taronga's 2026 Wild Deserts account reports successful survival/breeding following translocation.
