## Species identity and identification

Guineafowl typically have rounded bodies, spotted/dark plumage and relatively bare heads. Helmeted guineafowl are distinguished by a bony head casque, but 'guineafowl' on a historical zoo map is insufficient proof that this specific species was held.

**Native range / habitat:** Guineafowl as a family are African ground-dwelling birds, but different taxa occupy different habitats. Where the exhibit label lacks a confirmed scientific identification, this description is **conditional** on future species verification.

## Feeding, behaviour and natural history

Forage on the ground for seeds, bulbs, insects and other small foods; many guineafowl roost above ground and form flocks that communicate with sharp calls. Habitat needs differ by species.
