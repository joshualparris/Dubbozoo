## Identification, taxonomy and range

Large South Asian pheasant; displaying males have elongated, ocellated upper tail-coverts ('train') raised into a fan; the actual tail feathers are relatively short. Females have subtler plumage. Neither train length nor mating display alone proves an individual bird's age.

## Diet, behaviour and ecology

Forages for seeds, shoots, insects, small reptiles and other items; roosts above ground; males display to females during the breeding season. Introduced/free-ranging groups occur at many Australian institutions.

## Conservation ecology and threats

Not typically globally threatened; introduced peafowl may spread beyond grounds and cause local issues. Wild birds wandering into the zoo are not automatically managed animals.
