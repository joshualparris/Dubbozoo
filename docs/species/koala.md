# Koala — researched species guide

**Dubbozoo inventory #41 · Taxon:** *Phascolarctos cinereus* · **Conservation:** Australian EPBC Endangered in QLD/NSW/ACT populations (2022), IUCN taxonomy/status differs.  
**Sources reviewed:** 18 October 2026. **Observations:** no supplied photo directly documents this species.

## Physical identification and life history

Arboreal marsupial with distinctive large ears and nose, specialised gripping hands and variable fur thickness. Koalas are not bears, and a resting koala is not necessarily sick or bored.

## Food web, habitat and behavioural ecology

Highly specialised eucalyptus and related browse; selects certain trees/leaves while using gut fermentation to extract nutrition from fibre and tolerate many leaf defensive compounds. Usually solitary with overlapping ranges and loud breeding bellows.

**Field-research question:** How does specialised leaf digestion influence koala rest and the need for suitable eucalypts? Make notes about observable motion, feeding signs and substrate/habitat. Do not infer age, sex, stress or parentage without records.

## Risks and conservation actions

Habitat clearing, roads, dog attacks, climate heat/drought and disease including chlamydia. Habitat linkages and heat-safe refuges are central; healthy zoo animals do not mean wild koalas are recovering.

**Status caveat:** Australian EPBC Endangered in QLD/NSW/ACT populations (2022), IUCN taxonomy/status differs. National/state legal listings and IUCN global assessments may differ. Population totals from different places, years, age classes or releases cannot be combined into an invented present-day abundance estimate. Zoo conservation work should be judged by genetic diversity and wild survival outcomes, not only births.
