# Little lorikeet — researched species guide

**Dubbozoo inventory #42 · Taxon:** *Glossopsitta pusilla* · **Conservation:** NSW Vulnerable under state law; IUCN broader category may differ.  
**Sources reviewed:** 18 October 2026. **Observations:** no supplied photo directly documents this species.

## Physical identification and life history

Very small bright green parrot with red forehead and face, often seen high in flowering eucalyptus canopies. Smaller than scaly-breasted or rainbow lorikeets.

## Food web, habitat and behavioural ecology

Feeds on nectar and pollen of eucalypts and other flowering trees, and sometimes fruit/other plant products. Fast nomadic movements track flowering; nests often occupy tree hollows.

## Risks and conservation actions

Removal of old hollow-bearing trees, declining nectar resources, habitat fragmentation and extreme climate events, despite sometimes flying long distances.
