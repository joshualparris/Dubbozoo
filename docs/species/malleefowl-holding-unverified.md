## Physical identification and life history

Ground-dwelling mottled grey-brown bird with heavy feet. Famous for building large incubation mounds, not sitting on eggs continuously like most birds.

## Food web, habitat and behavioural ecology

A male manages the balance of decomposing vegetation, sand and solar heat in a mound to incubate eggs; chicks emerge able to fend for themselves. Mallee woodland leaf litter and suitable soil are essential.
