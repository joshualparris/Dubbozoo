## Identification, taxonomy and range

A wild Asian ass with sandy body, dark dorsal stripe and upright mane. Smaller ears than some wild asses and a different body build from Takhi/Przewalski's horses; photo labels should not be swapped.

Taxonomic names, local subspecies, domestic forms and conservation categories must align. Where the old zoo map uses a broad label or conflicting name, keep uncertainty until a dated Taronga animal-holdings record verifies the taxon.

## Diet, behaviour and ecology

Arid and semi-arid Iranian plains/deserts where it grazes and browses, using long travel to locate water and seasonal plants. Live in flexible groups; dry landscapes still need water and space for movement.

Research prompt: How can you distinguish a wild ass from a wild horse without relying solely on colour? When recording an observed animal, state the count, surroundings and directly seen behaviour rather than extrapolating kinship or medical condition.

## Conservation ecology and threats

Hunting, livestock competition, roads, fencing and loss of grazing/watering access; reintroduction programmes depend on protected connected areas.
