# Plains-wanderer — researched species guide

**Dubbozoo inventory #44 · Taxon:** *Pedionomus torquatus* · **Conservation:** Critically Endangered in Australia; IUCN category may be separately assessed.  
**Sources reviewed:** 18 October 2026. **Observations:** no supplied photo directly documents this species.

## Physical identification and life history

Unusual small ground bird of native Australian grasslands, with finely patterned buff-brown plumage and distinctive spotted/banded markings. A female is often more strikingly coloured; it is not a quail despite superficial resemblance.

## Food web, habitat and behavioural ecology

Feeds on seeds and invertebrates among sparse native grass clumps; maintains low vegetation cover for movement and shelter. Females can mate with multiple males and **males incubate eggs and care for chicks**, an unusual sex-role pattern.

**Field-research question:** What makes protection of native *grassland* different from rainforest protection? Make notes about observable motion, feeding signs and substrate/habitat. Do not infer age, sex, stress or parentage without records.

## Risks and conservation actions

Cropping, overgrazing, habitat fragmentation, intense wildfire and introduced predators. Conservation requires protecting the specific mosaic of sparse native grasses, not merely planting dense trees.
