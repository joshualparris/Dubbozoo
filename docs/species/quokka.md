# Quokka — researched species guide

## Physical identification and life history

Small rounded macropod with short face, powerful hindlegs, rounded ears and a relatively short coarse-furred tail. Its face can resemble a 'smile' but facial geometry alone is not proof of emotion.

## Food web, habitat and behavioural ecology

Browsing/grazing on vegetation, often nocturnal or crepuscular in natural habitats. Native to southwestern Western Australia and associated islands, inhabiting dense cover and using vegetation for shelter; it is not native to Dubbo.

## Risks and conservation actions

Fox and cat predation, drying climate, fire patterns and habitat fragmentation, especially mainland range. Rottnest's tourist-visible population is not a proxy for all wild quokka health.
