This confirms **temporary rehabilitation and on-site holding**, **not a permanent zoo breeding/exhibit collection**. The main collection still requires distinct confirmation.

## Physical identification and life history

Australia's largest wild macropod. Adult males often reddish, females frequently blue-grey, but colour overlaps and cannot always sex animals. Large hind limbs and tail support hopping locomotion.

## Food web, habitat and behavioural ecology

Feeds on grasses and forbs across inland arid/semi-arid Australia, moving with rainfall and fresh growth; reproduces through highly specialised marsupial lactation and embryonic development.

## Risks and conservation actions

Vehicle collisions, agricultural conflict and extreme drought influence local populations. Natural red kangaroos around Dubbo cannot be counted as captive holdings without actual inventory data.

**Status caveat:** Least Concern broadly. National/state legal listings and IUCN global assessments may differ. Population totals from different places, years, age classes or releases cannot be combined into an invented present-day abundance estimate. Zoo conservation work should be judged by genetic diversity and wild survival outcomes, not only births.
