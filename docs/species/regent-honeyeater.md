# Regent honeyeater — researched species guide

**Dubbozoo inventory #49 · Taxon:** *Anthochaera phrygia* · **Conservation:** Critically Endangered under Australian law.

## Physical identification and life history

Striking black-and-yellow honeyeater with pale scalloped wings and variable yellow cheek/eye markings. Bird leg bands may enable confirmed identity only when linked to Taronga's band register.

## Food web, habitat and behavioural ecology

Follows flowering eucalypts and mistletoes for nectar while also feeding on insects. Wild birds benefit from social learning of song; limited numbers of singing adults can affect communication and pairing.

## Risks and conservation actions

Severe woodland habitat loss, low population size, nest predation, drought and breakdown of learned song traditions; coordinated recovery combines habitat management, zoo breeding and releases followed by survival monitoring.

## Dubbo zoo's verified role / limitations

Taronga's Dubbo programme began in **2019/2020**; **41 chicks hatched in 2023** from nine pairs, cumulative 107 across three seasons. On **14 December 2023** it introduced **eight male birds** to public Wildlife Hospital aviary display. Its 2024–25 annual report lists **36 chicks born** in Dubbo. No release is claimed successful solely because zoo-bred animals were moved.
