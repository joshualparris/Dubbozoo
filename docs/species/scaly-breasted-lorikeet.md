## Scientific identification and adaptations

Small green lorikeet with yellow-green scaling across breast, red/orange bill and fast direct flight. Contrasts with little lorikeet by size and scale-patterned front; plumage viewing depends on light.

## Food, reproduction and ecological roles

Nectar and pollen feeding using a brush-tipped tongue, supplemented by some fruit and other seasonal foods. Follows flowering eucalypts, often using tree hollows for nesting. Nectarivores transport pollen among trees and can connect fragmented vegetation.

**Field question:** Can you distinguish tiny feather scaling from the uniform emerald colour of other lorikeets? Record time, location, number seen and directly observable actions without guessing health, social status or individual name.

## Threats, conservation and scientific limitations

Availability of flowering native trees and mature nesting hollows is important locally; land clearing and climate-driven flowering variability can reduce food predictability.

The assessment shown here is a dated conservation/legal context, **not a fresh population estimate**. Legal rankings in NSW, federal Australia and the IUCN may differ; wild, domestic, conservation-breeding and hospital animals must remain separate. For unknown taxonomy, no precise species-level Red List label should be assigned until confirmed.

## Specific Dubbo zoo history / verification status
