## Scientific identification and adaptations

Ten-legged crayfish with large strong claws, smooth-sided carapace and a broad swimming tail fan. Color can vary dramatically with habitat and genetics. 'Smooth yabby' is a local common name for *Cherax destructor*, but an old zoo list alone cannot tie it to a specific managed enclosure.

## Food, reproduction and ecological roles

Omnivorous detritivore eating plant fragments, dead material, small invertebrates and sometimes other crustaceans; helps recycle nutrients. Can burrow through drying periods, but needs appropriate water quality and oxygen when active; females carry eggs beneath the abdomen.

## Threats, conservation and scientific limitations

Waterway degradation, pollutants and inappropriate stocking/translocations can change native communities. Common yabbies are not automatically conservation programme animals.

The assessment shown here is a dated conservation/legal context, **not a fresh population estimate**.
