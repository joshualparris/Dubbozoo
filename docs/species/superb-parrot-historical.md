# Superb parrot — photographed in Dubbo's former walk-through aviary

**Scientific name:** *Polytelis swainsonii* · **Evidence:** dated public photographs of a bird described as inside the zoo aviary; not yet corroborated with a Taronga animal accession. **Research date:** 18 October 2026.

## Historical evidence

An independently licensed [photo on Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Polytelis_swainsonii_-Taronga_Western_Plains_Zoo,_near_Dubbo,_New_South_Wales,_Australia-8a.jpg) identifies an **adult male Superb Parrot** at **Taronga Western Plains Zoo**, photographed **15 October 2009** by Tobias Ziegler. It records camera coordinates within the zoo grounds and links its original Flickr source. A [2009 visitor photo travelogue](https://www.australiasomuchtosee.com/2009dubbozoo2.htm) explicitly captions a **Superb Parrot inside Dubbo's walk-through aviary**. Together these are strong evidence of a species seen in a visitor aviary in 2009, but individual identity or permanent formal collection status still awaits a keeper inventory. **Photographer evidence is not equivalent to government animal transaction records**.

## Identification, ecology and conservation

A graceful green parrot with long tail; mature males have a brighter yellow head and red throat/band, whereas females and immatures are more predominantly green. It is found in southeastern inland Australia, with breeding in large hollow-bearing river red gums and other mature woodland trees. Forages for seeds and plant material, travelling between breeding habitat and feeding areas. Clearing of nest trees, habitat fragmentation and landscape changes threaten local breeding areas. Check current EPBC/NSW legal listing and IUCN global listing separately, with exact taxon/year.
