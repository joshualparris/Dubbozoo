# Takhi (Przewalski's horse) — Dubbozoo species research

**Inventory number:** 34/61 · **Scientific identification:** *Equus ferus przewalskii (alternative full species Equus przewalskii)* · **Conservation:** Endangered; some assessments use alternative taxon ranks.  
**Research reviewed:** 18 October 2026. This is **not** a Josh-supplied photo observation.

## Identification, taxonomy and range

Compact wild horse with upright mane, dun-brown colouring and dark dorsal stripe. The ancestry and status of the last 'truly wild' horse lineage have scientific nuance; distinguish from feral descendants of domesticated horses.

Taxonomic names, local subspecies, domestic forms and conservation categories must align. Where the old zoo map uses a broad label or conflicting name, keep uncertainty until a dated Taronga animal-holdings record verifies the taxon.

## Diet, behaviour and ecology

Steppe grazers with tight social units and seasonal movement shaped by water, snow, grasses and avoidance of harsh conditions. Social structures include harem groups and bachelor bands.

Research prompt: How does reintroduction differ from simply increasing the number of zoo-born foals? When recording an observed animal, state the count, surroundings and directly seen behaviour rather than extrapolating kinship or medical condition.

## Conservation ecology and threats

Became extinct in the wild during the twentieth century; captive genetic management and international reintroductions to Mongolia restored populations, but small populations still face weather shocks, genetic bottlenecks and human disturbance.

**Assessment limit:** Endangered; some assessments use alternative taxon ranks. Wild population figures require a publication year and explicit denominator (mature breeders versus all ages). Zoo populations and reintroduced herds must not be added to wild estimates without a method that does so.

## Taronga Western Plains Zoo connection

Taronga's breeding programme dates back to **1982**. Its 2019 release records foal Tuya born 28 June to Genghis, and an old release says the programme contributed horses to reintroductions in Mongolia. The Wild Herds precinct confirms the species in contemporary Dubbo visitor material.

**Evidence policy:** published Taronga reports are dated records, not a live animal database. The exhibit location, keeper-talk time, current animal names, breeding status and count should be checked before a future visit; no personal sighting is asserted in this guide.
