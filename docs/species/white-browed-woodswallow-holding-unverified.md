## Scientific identification and adaptations

Small swallow-like insectivorous bird with white brow over dark face, pointed wings and short bill. A woodswallow is not a true swallow (different family), despite sharing aerial flight habits.

## Food, reproduction and ecological roles

Feeds on flying insects and often forms seasonal nomadic flocks. Can move in response to rains and insect abundance; colonial breeding and communal roosting vary with conditions.

**Field question:** Why can't a Taronga-wide species record be assumed to describe the Dubbo collection? Record time, location, number seen and directly observable actions without guessing health, social status or individual name.

## Threats, conservation and scientific limitations

Pesticides, loss of mature trees and reduction of insects can affect local populations, though the species is broadly distributed.

The assessment shown here is a dated conservation/legal context, **not a fresh population estimate**.
