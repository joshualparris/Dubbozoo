# White-handed gibbon (historical exhibit) — Dubbozoo species research

**Inventory number:** 35/61 · **Scientific identification:** *Hylobates lar* · **Conservation:** Endangered often reported, assessment year to verify.  
**Research reviewed:** 18 October 2026. This is **not** a Josh-supplied photo observation.

## Identification, taxonomy and range

Small tailless ape with long arms, hand/foot pale markings in many individuals and remarkable arm-over-arm brachiation. Coat colour can vary dramatically among individuals and sexes; coat shade alone is unreliable sex evidence.

Taxonomic names, local subspecies, domestic forms and conservation categories must align. Where the old zoo map uses a broad label or conflicting name, keep uncertainty until a dated Taronga animal-holdings record verifies the taxon.

## Diet, behaviour and ecology

Forest canopy frugivore with leaves and insects in the diet. Pairs and small family groups frequently defend ranges with complex vocal duets; flexible social arrangements and dispersal occur.

Research prompt: How do gibbon family songs differ from siamang calls, and can silent photos prove singing? When recording an observed animal, state the count, surroundings and directly seen behaviour rather than extrapolating kinship or medical condition.

## Conservation ecology and threats

Deforestation, fragmentation, hunting and illegal trade threaten wild gibbons. Canopy gaps disrupt travel, food and mate access.

**Assessment limit:** Endangered often reported, assessment year to verify. Wild population figures require a publication year and explicit denominator (mature breeders versus all ages). Zoo populations and reintroduced herds must not be added to wild estimates without a method that does so.

## Taronga Western Plains Zoo connection

Dubbo historic map records a **White-handed Gibbon island**. Taronga's 2011 ring-tailed lemur announcement locates a bachelor group near that island. Contemporary siamang maps should not be mistaken as proof that white-handed gibbons remain there now.

**Evidence policy:** published Taronga reports are dated records, not a live animal database. The exhibit location, keeper-talk time, current animal names, breeding status and count should be checked before a future visit; no personal sighting is asserted in this guide.
