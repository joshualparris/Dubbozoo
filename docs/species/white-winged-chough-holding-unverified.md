## Scientific identification and adaptations

Large black bird with curved bill, distinctive red eyes and hidden white wing panels visible in flight. Not a crow or a magpie; belongs to a different Australian bird lineage.

## Food, reproduction and ecological roles

Forages in groups on the ground, probing soil and leaf litter for invertebrates. Complex cooperative breeding may include helpers at nests, and groups use mud to build large cup-shaped nests.
