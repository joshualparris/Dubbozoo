The original question atlas sometimes marked answerable biology “unresolved”. The revised atlas gives every parent question a response, then separates sourced findings from facts still requiring keeper, archival or photographic evidence.

05 / OPEN NOTEBOOK

Follow the
evidence trail.

Research links are more trustworthy when you can see how each conclusion was reached, how old the source is, and what it cannot prove.

01

Species ≠ animal ID

A documented species does not establish an individual’s name, age, genetics or whether it is visible today. Historical zoo occupancy is not current availability.

02

IUCN status ≠ trend

Global extinction risk, wild abundance trend, Australian legislation and captive zoo counts are different measures, each with its own year and taxonomic scope.

03

Unknown means unknown

Visitor journals and planned exhibits are useful leads, but not always confirmed accessions. Conflicting labels and original photo privacy are retained as evidence boundaries.
