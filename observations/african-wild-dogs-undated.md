# Field observation — two African wild dogs, undated photographs

**Provisional record:** `dubbozoo-obs-0001` · **Species:** *Lycaon pictus* · **Image count:** 5 · **Research recorded:** 2026-10-18.

## Evidence and limitations

This record is based on five photos supplied by Josh during a conversation about `Dubbozoo`. The images visually depict **two African wild dogs**, in a grassy, tree-lined, maintained animal habitat. A pane of visitor glass and a person holding a bicycle are visible in the widest image. The **location is consistent with Taronga Western Plains Zoo Dubbo** and its African wild dog enclosure, but there are **no native camera/GPS/date EXIF tags** in these uploaded copies.

**Never equate the research date with the visit date.** The names, sex, individual identities, age, season, time of day, and exact enclosure GPS coordinates **have not been verified**.

The two dogs have distinct patches of black, tan and white, large upright ears, narrow builds and pale tail tips. We cannot prove that all photographs document the same two individuals, although the continuous visual context strongly suggests a single short observation sequence.
