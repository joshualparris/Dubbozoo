## Behaviour interpretation

A 2024 study of 66 giraffes at 18 accredited U.S. zoos found feeding/foraging was the most common daytime behaviour category, but individual patterns varied substantially. A 2025 follow-up examined how management and environmental factors relate to giraffe behaviour. These studies are useful for an observation vocabulary, **not** for diagnosing the Dubbo giraffes from still photographs.

For this record, “head at elevated feeder” is safer than “hungry”, “dominant”, “stressed” or “always fed this way”. Repeated observation and keeper/contextual data would be needed for those interpretations.
