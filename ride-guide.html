Location, privacy & accuracy

GPS is opt-in: The location API runs on this device while the page is open. We do not transmit your current coordinates to GitHub, Vercel, YouTube or Wikimedia, and we do not run analytics on the ride guide. Only viewing spots you explicitly save are kept in browser storage.

Distance is approximate: Each listed distance is straight-line from your phone to an OpenStreetMap community pin or your saved viewing spot. GPS can drift among trees. A map pin does not prove an animal is in that exact spot, visible today or a specific named individual.

Photo geodata: We audited the exact supplied JPEG copies used by the field journal. None retained EXIF, GPS or camera capture time. The photos therefore strengthen animal/exhibit evidence only; precise map coordinates still require an independent GPS/map source or a viewing point saved on site.

Other media: Wildlife photos load from Wikimedia Commons with author and licence credits. Genuine Taronga footage plays in an embedded privacy-enhanced YouTube player only after you tap; YouTube then receives a network request. You can also open the original video on YouTube. The on-device audio stories are original short narration, not real keeper podcasts.

Ride safely: Use designated one-way routes, stop before interacting, observe zoo rules, and use the official Taronga app for directions and current events.
